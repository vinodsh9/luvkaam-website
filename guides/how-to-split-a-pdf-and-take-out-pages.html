How to split a PDF and pull out the pages you need

You have a 40-page PDF and need pages 5 to 8. Or a scanned file with one page too many. Or each page must go to a different person. All of that is splitting.

Steps

  1. Open Split PDF.
  2. Choose your PDF.
  3. Pick what you want under Split by (see below).
  4. Type the pages if the mode needs them.
  5. Press Split file and download.

The three modes

Ranges (one file each). Each range you type becomes its own PDF. Typing 1-3, 4-6 gives you two files. If there is more than one file, you get them together in one zip.

Extract pages into one file. The pages you type are collected into a single new PDF. This is the one to use for "I only need pages 5 to 8".

Every page. Each page becomes its own PDF. Good for forms where every page goes somewhere different.

How to type the pages

  • 1-3 means pages 1, 2 and 3.
  • 5 means only page 5.
  • 1-3, 5, 8- means pages 1 to 3, then page 5, then page 8 to the end. A dash with nothing after it means "to the last page".

Use commas between the parts. If you type a page that does not exist, you will see a message.

Removing pages you do not want

There is no "delete page" button. Do it the other way round: use Extract pages and type every page you want to keep. To drop page 4 of a 10-page file, type 1-3, 5-10.

Putting parts back together

Use Merge PDF to join the parts again, in any order. This is also how you move a page: split it out, then merge the pieces in the new order.

Good to know

  • The original file is never changed. You always get new files.
  • Splitting happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • A PDF with a password has to be opened with Unlock PDF first.
  • Very long scans can be cut into parts before reading them with OCR scanned PDF, which reads 20 pages at a time.

How to convert a screenshot to text (copy text from an image)

Updated 23 October 2026

A screenshot is a picture, so you cannot select or copy the words in it. That is a problem when the screenshot holds a long message, an error, a table of figures or a page that blocks copying. Optical character recognition (OCR) reads the picture and gives you the words back as text.

Why screenshots work well

Screen text is sharp, evenly lit and usually set in a clean font, which is exactly what OCR handles best. A screenshot almost always beats a photo of a screen taken with a phone camera.

Steps

  1. Take the screenshot at normal size. Do not zoom out, because tiny letters are harder to read.
  2. Crop it to the block of text you want and save it as PNG or JPG.
  3. Open the Image to Text tool on Luvkaam and choose the file.
  4. Run the conversion, then copy the result.
  5. Read it once against the screenshot and fix any mistakes.

Tips for common cases

  • Chat messages: capture one conversation at a time and crop out names and profile pictures.
  • Tables: expect the columns to collapse into lines. Check every number.
  • Dark mode: light text on a dark background can lower accuracy. Switch to a light theme before you capture if you can.
  • Long pages: capture in sections and convert each one, so the text stays large.

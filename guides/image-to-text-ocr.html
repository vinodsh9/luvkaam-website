How to convert an image to text online (free OCR)

Updated 23 October 2026

You have a photo of a notice, a screenshot of an email or a picture of a printed page, and you need the words as text you can copy and edit. The technology that does this is called OCR, short for optical character recognition.

What OCR does

OCR looks at the shapes in a picture and matches them to letters and numbers. It does not understand the meaning of a page, so it works best on clear, printed text and struggles with handwriting, decorative fonts and blurry photos.

Steps

  1. Open the Image to Text tool on Luvkaam.
  2. Choose your image. JPG, PNG and WEBP files are supported.
  3. Start the conversion and wait while the text is recognised in your browser.
  4. Read through the result, fix any mistakes, and copy the text where you need it.

English text is supported at the moment. If your image is in another language, expect poor results.

Get a better result before you start

  • Photograph the page straight on, not at an angle.
  • Use even light and avoid shadows across the text.
  • Crop away borders and anything that is not text.
  • Prefer a sharp screenshot to a photo of a screen.

We cover this in more detail in How to improve OCR accuracy.

Always proofread

OCR can confuse similar characters, such as the letter O and the number 0, or the letter l and the number 1. Check names, dates, phone numbers and amounts against the original before you use them.

Your image stays on your device

Luvkaam's tools are designed to process images in your browser, so the picture is not uploaded to our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

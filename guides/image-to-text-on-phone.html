How to convert an image to text on Android and iPhone

Updated 23 October 2026

Most photos of documents are taken on a phone, so it helps to convert them without moving to a computer. You can do it in your mobile browser with nothing to install.

Steps on any phone

  1. Open luvkaam.com in Chrome, Safari or any other browser.
  2. Go to the Image to Text tool.
  3. Tap to choose a file. Pick a photo from your gallery, or take a new one if your browser offers the camera.
  4. Run the conversion and wait for the text to appear.
  5. Press and hold the result to select it, then copy it into your notes or a message.

Take a better photo

  • Hold the phone parallel to the page, not at an angle.
  • Use daylight and avoid your own shadow on the paper.
  • Tap the screen to focus before you shoot.
  • Crop to the text so the tool reads less clutter.

More ideas are in our guide to improving OCR accuracy.

Built-in options

Many phones can already read text from photos. iPhones have Live Text in the Photos app and the camera, and many Android phones offer a similar feature through Google Lens. A web tool is still useful when you want to work on a computer, handle many files, or prefer to keep to one workflow.

Privacy on mobile

Luvkaam's tools are designed to process your image in your browser, so the picture is not uploaded to our servers. Read our Privacy Policy for details, and avoid using sensitive documents on public Wi-Fi.

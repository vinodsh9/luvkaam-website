Are online PDF and image tools safe?

Updated 23 October 2026

Free online tools are convenient, but the file you open may be an Aadhaar copy, a salary slip or a contract. Before you use any tool, spend a minute on this checklist.

1. Find out whether your file is uploaded

Many tools send your file to a server to process it. Others process it inside your browser, so the file never leaves your device. Look for a clear statement on the site, and read the privacy policy if there is none. If you cannot tell, treat the file as uploaded.

2. Check the page is secure

The address should start with https and show a padlock. Do not open sensitive files on a page that does not.

3. Think before you share identity and money documents

Passports, ID cards, bank statements and tax papers are valuable to fraudsters. If a tool does not say how it handles files, use something else or hide the sensitive parts first.

4. Remove what you do not need

Crop out account numbers, signatures and addresses that are not relevant. Photos can also carry hidden location data, so strip it where you can.

5. Avoid public computers

If you must use a shared computer, clear downloads and close the browser tab when you finish.

6. Read what a free site gets in return

Free services are paid for by ads, data or subscriptions. A clear privacy policy that names its ad and hosting partners is a good sign. Be wary of sites that ask for more personal details than they need.

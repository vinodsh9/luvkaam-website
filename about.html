About Luvkaam

Luvkaam is a set of free tools for everyday PDF and photo jobs. You can merge, split, rotate, compress, sign, lock and unlock PDFs. You can turn photos into PDFs, convert image formats, and pull text out of images and scans. That includes Hindi.

Why we made it

Most free PDF sites ask you to upload your file to their server. For a college form that is fine. For a bank statement or an ID card, many people are not comfortable with it. So we built tools that run inside your own browser. Your file stays on your phone or laptop while you work on it.

What it can and cannot do

  • It works best on normal documents and clear photos.
  • PDF to Word keeps the text, not the design.
  • PDF to Excel is a best guess for simple tables.
  • Reading text from images is good on clear printed pages and weak on handwriting.
  • The signature tool adds a picture of your signature. It is not a certificate-based digital signature.

We say this plainly on each tool page too, so you know what to expect before you start.

What it costs

The tools are free. Merge, Compress, JPG to PDF and Image to text can be used three times a day without an account. A free registration removes that limit and opens all 16 tools. We may show ads on the site in future to pay for it. If you register and agree to it, we may also send you offers. You can say no, and you can withdraw at any time.

Who runs it

Luvkaam is run from Gurgaon, Haryana, India. You can write to us from the Contact page or at hello@luvkaam.com. Our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use explain what we collect and the rules for using the site.

Tell us what is missing

If a tool fails on your file, or you need one we do not have, tell us. Real problems from real files are how the tools get better.

About Luvkaam

Luvkaam is a free toolkit for everyday document work: pulling text out of images and PDFs today, with more tools on the way. It is built for students, job seekers, small businesses and anyone who needs a quick result without installing software.

Who runs it

Luvkaam is built and run in Gurgaon, Haryana, India. We make simple, free document tools, such as image to text and PDF to text, that work in your browser so your files stay on your device. We publish plain-language guides to go with each tool, and we read every message sent to our support address.

What you can do today

  • Image to Text: read text from JPG, PNG and WEBP images using OCR. English is supported now.
  • PDF to Text: extract text from PDFs that contain selectable text.

Other tools shown on the site are in development and will be switched on as they are ready. Our guides explain how to get the best results from the tools that are live.

Our approach to privacy

The tools are designed to run in your browser, so your files stay on your device. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

How the site is funded

Luvkaam is free to use and supported by advertising. We keep the ads separate from the tools and never ask you to pay to download your own results.
